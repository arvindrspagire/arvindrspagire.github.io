**Summary:** (sample text) Because of a low-pressure area over the Bay of Bengal, heavy rain is likely at a few places in east Vidarbha in the next 24 hours.

### Next 24 hours
- Moderate to heavy rain at most places in east Vidarbha.
- Thunderstorms with lightning from the afternoon.

### Advice for citizens
- Do not stand under trees or in open fields during lightning.
- Do not drive through flood water in rivers and streams.
- Farmers should keep harvested crops in a safe place.

| District | Expected rain | Alert |
|---|---|---|
| Nagpur | Moderate to heavy | Orange |
| Wardha | Moderate | Yellow |
